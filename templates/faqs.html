About Betten AI

Betten AI is a local, uncensored AI assistant designed for creative minds. It runs entirely on your machine, ensuring privacy and security.

This project was created by Nox, a passionate developer focused on building tools that empower creativity without compromising privacy.

What is Betten AI?

Betten AI is an AI companion that helps you with creative ideas, coding, writing, and more. It's designed to be uncensored and helpful for all kinds of tasks.

Is my data safe?

Yes! Betten AI runs locally on your machine using Ollama. No data is sent to external servers, and chat history is only stored temporarily in your browser session.

How do I use it?

Simply type your questions or requests in the chat interface. Betten AI will respond with helpful, formatted answers including code blocks when appropriate.

Can I start new conversations?

Yes, you can start new chats from the sidebar. Each conversation is independent, and you can switch between them easily.

Enjoy using Betten AI!

Have fun exploring your creativity with Betten AI. Whether you're coding, writing, or brainstorming ideas, Betten is here to help you build amazing things.
